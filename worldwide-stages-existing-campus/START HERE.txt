START WITH THE BLENDER FILE
Worldwide Stages - Existing Campus.blend is the editable master.
It preserves native geometry, procedural materials, lighting,
three presentation cameras plus a survey overview camera.
Built with Blender 4.5 LTS, in meters. No external textures needed.

Three PNGs show the Blender model at 3,840 x 2,400 pixels.

The two GLB files are portable copies for other 3D applications.
Their material appearance is simpler than the Blender master.
Building Only.glb excludes the grounds and landscape.

References contains original-resolution source images.
Viewer compares Blender renders and a rotatable GLB with those sources.
The viewer is in the Viewer folder.

Geometry comes from a revised 2021 survey and 2018 aerial scan.
Individual facade details are interpretations, not a measured
current as-built. Read Source notes.txt before relying on dimensions.
