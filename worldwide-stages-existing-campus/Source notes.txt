WHAT THE MODEL REPRESENTS

The existing connected campus only. No proposed soundstages,
data centers, outdoor crowds or future construction are included.
This is an exterior visualization, not a current measured as-built,
construction model, interior model or verified property boundary.

SOURCES AND ACCURACY

Building outline: vector topographic survey revised in 2021,
with its original 100 feet per inch scale. The modeled footprint
is approximately 18,283 square meters; this is footprint area,
not the property's reported gross building area.

Roof tiers: fitted to February 27, 2018 airborne LiDAR returns.
Small classification slivers at exterior roof edges were consolidated
within 0.12 meters; source roof levels were retained.
The survey-to-scan alignment is a visually supported candidate;
its full survey datum/foot convention has not been established.
Elevations are expressed in a local display frame with 225 meters
subtracted from NAVD88. Do not treat display Z as finished-floor
height above ground or use it for construction.

Appearance: operator's Building 400 directions aerial (PDF
created December 2022, published January 2023; photograph date
unknown), the January 2023 operator press kit, and supplied assets.

Source-derived: building perimeter, broad roof tiers, canopy
location, historical terrain, native parking-line locations and
historical tree-canopy locations.

Interpreted: individual facade openings and mullions, parapet
thickness, atrium roof framing, canopy rounding, rooftop equipment
shapes, some pavement and sidewalk connections, pond edge finish,
vegetation forms, parking lights, cars and material appearance.
These details are visual approximations, not measured drawings.
Neighboring properties and complete road surroundings are omitted.

No 100-foot stage height or future expansion was assumed.
